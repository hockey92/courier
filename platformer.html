Cowboy Run

Ride through the zombie-infested frontier and reach the saloon at the end of the trail.

Move: A / D or arrow keys  ·  Jump: Space, W or Up  ·  Shoot: J or X

Stomp or shoot the zombies, grab coins, and hit the ? blocks from below. Mind the cacti and the pits.

M mutes all sound, N turns the music on or off.
