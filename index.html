Zombie Maze

You are lost in an endless frontier town overrun by zombies. You shoot automatically at any zombie you can see.

They shuffle in from beyond every corner. Move with WASD or the arrow keys.

Grab the gold sheriff stars to fire more bullets at once. Press M to mute all sound, or N to turn the music on or off.
